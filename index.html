About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Physics and Head of the Department of Physics at Debre Berhan University, Ethiopia. My research focuses on observational astrophysics, particularly classical and recurrent novae, cataclysmic variable stars, and interacting binaries. I combine multi-wavelength observations with photoionization and 3D morpho-kinematic modeling to investigate the physical evolution of nova eruptions.

Research Interests

  • Classical and recurrent novae
  • Optical and near-infrared spectroscopy
  • Multi-wavelength photometry
  • Photoionization modeling (CLOUDY)
  • 3D morpho-kinematic modeling (SHAPE)
  • Cataclysmic variable stars
